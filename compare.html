DMT vs. Ayahuasca vs. the Others

How DMT sits among its relatives — and why the same molecule can feel like two different worlds depending on how it's taken.

Start here

DMT and ayahuasca are the same molecule — sort of

This trips people up constantly. The core psychedelic in both is DMT. The difference is delivery, and delivery changes everything.

Smoked / vaporized DMT

Pure DMT, inhaled. Onset in seconds, peak overwhelming, over in 5–20 minutes. A sudden “breakthrough” catapult — often solitary and startlingly abrupt.

Ayahuasca (drunk)

DMT plus an MAO-inhibiting vine that makes it orally active. Slow onset, a 4–6 hour arc, purging, and a traditionally ceremonial, guided container. Same molecule, a very different journey.

Curious why the vine is needed? That's the MAO-inhibitor story on the science page.

Reading the differences

What actually sets them apart

01

Duration is a safety factor

A hard moment on DMT passes in minutes; the same moment on LSD can last hours. Longer trips demand more preparation, support and stamina.

02

Character, not just intensity

DMT is visionary and other-worldly; psilocybin is earthy and emotional; ketamine is detached and dreamlike. They are not interchangeable.

03

Different chemistry, different rules

Serotonergic psychedelics (DMT, psilocybin, LSD) share a mechanism and low dependence risk. Ketamine is a dissociative and can be habit-forming.

04

Container matters most

Ayahuasca's traditional ceremony, the clinic's guided session, or a chaotic party are three different worlds around the very same chemistry.

A special word on 5-MeO-DMT

Often confused with DMT, 5-MeO-DMT is a distinct and considerably more potent compound (famously associated with the Sonoran Desert toad). Its dosing window is narrow, the ego-dissolution can be total and frightening, and there have been serious adverse events. It deserves even more caution than DMT, not less.

The interaction that hospitalises people

Because ayahuasca contains an MAO inhibitor, combining it with SSRIs, SNRIs, other MAOIs, certain foods, or stimulants can trigger serotonin syndrome or a hypertensive crisis — medical emergencies. Never combine psychedelics with medications without expert medical guidance.

Different doorways, different rooms. Knowing which is which is the beginning of respect.
